In addition to the purposes the License permits, you may use the software for the
internal business purposes of your company, as long as your company is a Small Business.

"Small Business" means that, for the most recent completed tax year, your company,
together with all organizations that control it, are under its control, or are under
common control with it:

- had fewer than 100 individuals working for it as employees or independent contractors,
  counted together; and

  revenue.

An individual working on their own account (for example, a freelancer or sole proprietor)
who meets these limits is a Small Business.

Internal business purposes include your company's employees and contractors using the
software for their own work for your company, such as dictating text and recording and
summarizing your company's meetings.
