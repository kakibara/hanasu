## Who can use Hanasu for free

| Who / how | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Personal, noncommercial use (hobby, study, personal research) | Free |
| Educational institutions, public research organizations, charities, government institutions | Free |
| Internal business use by a small business (see below; includes freelancers and sole proprietors) | Free |
| Any company evaluating whether to buy a commercial license (30 days per company) | Free |

A **small business** is a company that, together with its affiliates, had in the most recent
tax year both:

- fewer than 100 employees and independent contractors in total, and
- less than 1,000,000 US dollars in revenue.

If your company stops being a small business, you can keep using Hanasu for 90 days after the
end of that tax year.

## When you need a commercial license

- A company larger than a small business uses Hanasu for work (after the 30-day evaluation).
- A company of any size sells Hanasu or a modified version of it, builds it into a product or
  service, or offers it as a hosted service.

If you are not sure which applies to you, ask us through the form below.

## What the commercial license covers

- **Counting:** by the number of people who actually use Hanasu. One person counts once, however
  many Macs or Windows PCs they use. We do not count your whole headcount.
- **Term:** yearly.
- **Price:** please contact us. Volume discounts are available.
- **Payment:** quotes and invoices are available.
- **AI costs:** Hanasu uses your own Gemini, Claude or Vertex AI API key, or a local model on
  your device. AI usage is not included in the commercial license; you pay the AI provider
  directly. With your company's Vertex AI, text is processed inside your own Google Cloud. With a
  local model, text never leaves your device (each model has its own license from its publisher).
